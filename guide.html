12 On-Chain Signals — Quick Reference
Covered in depth in Guide 1 · Risk Scanner
Mint Authority
Critical
Can the dev mint unlimited tokens? Revoked = safe.
Freeze Authority
Critical
Can the dev freeze your tokens? Revoked = safe.
Liquidity Pool
High
LP locked or burned? Unlocked LP = rug pull risk.
Top 10 Holders
High
Whales holding too much? Dump risk if concentrated.
Bundle Score
Medium
Was launch coordinated? High bundle = suspicious launch.
Dev Activity
Medium
Did the dev sell already? Past rugs? Track their history.
MC / LIQ Ratio
Medium
Market cap vs pool depth. >100× is danger territory.
Volume / MC
Medium
Is volume real relative to market cap? Or wash-traded?
Liquidity (USD)
Standard
Raw pool depth. Below $10K = thin ice.
Holder Count
Standard
Fewer than 100 holders? Very early — very risky.
Buy Pressure
Standard
24h buy vs sell ratio. Bearish divergence = exit signal.
Pump Danger
Standard
Extreme 1H gain with low liquidity = pump & dump pattern.
