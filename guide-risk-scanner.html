Finding a Token's Mint Address

Before you can scan a token, you need its mint address. Think of it as the token's unique fingerprint on the Solana blockchain — it never changes and there can only be one.

What is a mint address? Every Solana token has a unique mint address — a 32–44 character code made of letters and numbers. It's sometimes called the "Contract Address" or "CA". This is what Scan2Moon uses to look up all on-chain data for that token.
Example mint address
BBKPiLM9KjdJW7oQSKt99RVWcZdhF6sEHRKnwqeBGHST

How to find a token's mint address:

  1. Go to DexScreener.com (the most reliable source for Solana token data)
  2. Search for the token by name — for example type "GHOST" in the search bar
  3. Click on the correct token result (check the symbol and pair carefully)
  4. Look for "Contract" or "CA" on the token page — this is the mint address
  5. Copy the full address and paste it into the Scan2Moon search bar
Never use the token name to scan Anyone can create a fake token called "GHOST" or "SOL". Always use the actual mint address to make sure you're scanning the real token, not an impostor.
Token mint vs. wallet address A wallet address is YOUR address on Solana. A mint address is the TOKEN's address. They look similar but are completely different. Pasting your own wallet address into Scan2Moon will not work — it needs a token mint.

Reading the Risk Score

Every scan produces a single number from 0 to 100. This is the Risk Score — the higher the number, the safer the token looks based on its on-chain signals.

80–100
MOON COIN
Strong signals, healthy liquidity, low rug risk.
65–79
LOW RISK
Solid picture. Watch for any weak individual signals.
45–64
MODERATE
Mixed signals. Trade carefully and size your position down.
25–44
HIGH RISK
Multiple red flags. Not recommended unless you know the project well.
0–24
EXTREME
Serious danger signs. Avoid unless you are prepared to lose everything.
How is the score calculated? Scan2Moon checks 12 independent signals and weights each one based on importance. No single signal can inflate your score — the system requires clean signals across the board to score high.
The score is a tool, not a guarantee A high score reduces risk — it does not eliminate it. Always trade with money you can afford to lose, regardless of scan results.

The 12 Signals — What They Mean

Below the Risk Score you'll see a grid of 12 signals. Each one scores 0–100 individually. Here's what each signal measures and why it matters:

Token Age Trust
14%
How old is the token? Brand-new tokens (under 30 mins) score very low — there's no track record yet.
Market Integrity
12%
Is the price crashing? A token down 65%+ in 24h scores near zero — someone is selling hard.
Pump Danger
9%
Detects pump-and-dump setup: extreme price spike with weak volume or fast reversal.
LP Strength
8%
How much real money is in the liquidity pool? Below $5K means a single sell can crash the price.
MC / Liq Ratio
8%
Market Cap ÷ Liquidity. If the ratio is 1000× or higher, the price cannot be sustained.
Sell Pressure
8%
How many people are selling vs. buying right now? High sell pressure signals a possible price collapse.
Dev Behavior
7%
What is the developer wallet doing? Active mint authority means they can print unlimited tokens.
Bundle Attack Score
8%
Were there bot-coordinated buys at launch? Bundle attackers buy in bulk, inflate price, then dump.
Pump.fun Launch Risk
12%
Still on the bonding curve? Tokens on pump.fun have "virtual" liquidity — not real exit liquidity.
LP Stability
5%
Is the liquidity pool growing, stable, or shrinking? A rapidly draining pool is a red flag.
Volume Consistency
4%
Sudden massive volume spikes followed by silence often indicate wash trading or artificial hype.
Vol / MCap Ratio
5%
Volume relative to market cap. Extreme values suggest manipulation in either direction.
Which signals matter most? The three signals with the biggest weight are Token Age Trust (14%), Market Integrity (12%), and Pump.fun Launch Risk (12%). LP Strength is lower weight but acts as a hard override — extremely low liquidity caps the total score regardless of other signals.

Red Flags — When to Walk Away

Even if you're excited about a token, these signals should give you serious pause. Skipping a bad trade is just as profitable as catching a good one.

🔴
Score below 30 — Extreme RiskMultiple systems are flagging danger. The on-chain data is warning you clearly.
🔴
LP Strength under 30 / 100Very weak liquidity. One whale sell can wipe the pool. You may not be able to exit.
🔴
Freeze Authority: ActiveThe developer can freeze your token balance and block you from selling at any time.
🟡
Token under 10 minutes oldBrand-new tokens have no track record. Bots and bundlers are most active in the first few minutes.
🟡
Pump.fun Launch Risk below 40Token is still on the bonding curve. Check if it has graduated to Raydium or Meteora.
🔴
Bundle Attack Score below 40Coordinated bot buys at launch were detected. You are likely buying from bundlers right now.
🟡
Dev Holdings above 15%The developer controls a large portion of supply. A single sell can crash the price significantly.
🟡
Top 10 holders above 70%A handful of wallets own most of the supply. Coordinated dumping can crash the price instantly.
The discipline rule If three or more red flags appear in a single scan, consider it a hard pass regardless of the hype around that token.
