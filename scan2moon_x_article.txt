🔍 What is Scan2Moon?

Scan2Moon is a web-based risk intelligence dashboard built specifically for the Solana blockchain. You paste a token address, and within seconds you get a full picture of what's really going on behind the scenes — liquidity health, whale activity, holder distribution, market signals, and a clear overall Risk Score from 0 to 100.

Think of it as your on-chain due diligence assistant — always available, always objective, and completely free to use.

No sign-up. No wallet connection needed to scan. Just go to scan2moon.com, paste a token address, and let the scanner do the work.

⚡ Feature #1 — Risk Scanner

The heart of Scan2Moon. Paste any Solana token address and instantly get:

🟢 Risk Score (0–100) — one clear number that tells you how risky a token is
💧 Liquidity Analysis — is there enough liquidity to actually exit your position?
🐋 Whale Concentration — how much is held by a small group of wallets?
👥 Holder Distribution — healthy spread or dangerous top-heavy structure?
📊 Market Signals — volume trends, price momentum, and early warning flags
🔐 Contract Signals — mint authority, freeze authority, and ownership checks
